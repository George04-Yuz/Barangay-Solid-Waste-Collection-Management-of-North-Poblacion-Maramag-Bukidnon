# North Poblacion CleanTrack
## Resident Android APK Download & Manual Installation Guide

This guide provides simple, step-by-step instructions for residents, barangay officials, and collection personnel of **Barangay North Poblacion, Maramag, Bukidnon** to download and install the official **North Poblacion CleanTrack** mobile application (`.apk`) directly onto their Android smartphones.

---

## 📋 System Requirements

| Specification | Requirement |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Operating System** | Android 7.0 (Nougat, API 24) or higher |
| **Supported Devices** | Any Android smartphone or tablet (Samsung, Xiaomi, Oppo, Vivo, Realme, Google Pixel, etc.) |
| **Storage Required** | ~35 MB free space |
| **Internet Access** | Required for initial APK download and real-time cloud updates (offline demo supported) |
| **Official Package Name**| `ph.northpoblacion.cleantrack` |
| **Current Release File** | `North-Poblacion-CleanTrack-v1.0.0.apk` |

---

## 🚀 Step-by-Step Installation Instructions

### Step 1: Download the Official APK
1. Open your mobile web browser (e.g., Google Chrome) on your Android phone.
2. Navigate to the project's official GitHub Releases page:
   `https://github.com/north-poblacion-cleantrack/releases` (or your repository link)
3. Under the latest **v1.0.0** release, find the **Assets** section.
4. Tap on **`North-Poblacion-CleanTrack-v1.0.0.apk`** to begin downloading.
5. If Google Chrome shows a warning saying *"File might be harmful. Do you want to download North-Poblacion-CleanTrack-v1.0.0.apk anyway?"*, tap **Download anyway**.
   > *Note: Android displays this standard message for any application downloaded outside the Google Play Store.*

---

### Step 2: Open the Downloaded APK File
1. Once the download finishes, tap **Open** in the browser notification.
2. If you missed the notification, open your phone's **Files** or **Downloads** app, find `North-Poblacion-CleanTrack-v1.0.0.apk`, and tap on it.

---

### Step 3: Allow "Install Unknown Apps" (One-Time Setup)
Because this application is distributed directly by the Barangay project rather than the commercial Google Play Store, Android requires you to authorize the installation source:

1. A popup will appear saying:
   > *"For your security, your phone is not allowed to install unknown apps from this source."*
2. Tap **Settings** on that popup.
3. Toggle the switch next to **Allow from this source** to **ON** (Blue or Green).
4. Tap the **Back arrow (<)** at the top left to return to the installer.

---

### Step 4: Install the Application
1. Tap **Install** at the bottom right.
2. The installation process takes approximately 5 to 10 seconds.
3. Once completed, tap **Open** to launch **North Poblacion CleanTrack**!

---

### Step 5: Google Play Protect Dialog (If Prompted)
Depending on your phone manufacturer, a Google Play Protect scan window may appear:
- If prompted with *"Unrecognized app"*, tap **More details** and select **Install anyway**.
- **Do NOT disable Google Play Protect.** You only need to approve this specific official barangay APK.

## 🛠️ Demonstration & Operational Modes

Once installed, **North Poblacion CleanTrack** is ready for immediate use:

1. **Top Demonstration Switcher**:
   - Use the top pill toolbar to switch between **Admin**, **Worker (Driver)**, and **Resident** personas during capstone evaluations.
2. **Select Any of the 18 Puroks**:
   - Tap your Purok chip (Purok 1 through 10, including 1A, 1B, 2A, 5A, 5B, 6A, 9A, 9B) to view custom pickup schedules and notices.
3. **Report Missed Waste**:
   - Tap the red **"Report Missed Waste"** button to file complaints with location details and photo attachments.
4. **Backend Connection Settings**:
   - Tap the **(i)** Info icon on the top right to access the About screen.
   - You can toggle between **Embedded Live Demo Mode** (pre-seeded with all 18 puroks for offline defense) and **Production HTTPS Server Mode** (`https://api.northpoblacion.gov.ph/api/v1`).

## ❓ Troubleshooting & FAQs

### Problem 1: "App Not Installed" or "Package Appears Corrupted"
- **Cause 1:** Incomplete download caused by network interruption.
  - *Fix:* Delete the downloaded `.apk` file from your Downloads folder and download it again over a stable Wi-Fi connection.
- **Cause 2:** An older version of the app is already installed with a conflicting test signature.
  - *Fix:* Uninstall the existing version from your home screen, then tap `North-Poblacion-CleanTrack-v1.0.0.apk` again to install clean.

### Problem 2: "Parse Error: There was a problem parsing the package"
- **Cause:** Your Android version is below Android 7.0 (API 24), or the file download was corrupted.
  - *Fix:* Ensure your device is running Android 7.0 Nougat or newer in `Settings > About phone > Android version`.

### Problem 3: "Insufficient Storage Space"
- **Cause:** Your device has less than 50 MB of free storage.
  - *Fix:* Clear unused temporary cache files or media to free at least 100 MB, then retry installation.

### Problem 4: Cannot Connect to Production Server
- **Cause:** The live cloud server is undergoing maintenance or the phone has no internet connection.
  - *Fix:* Open the **(i)** Info modal and toggle the switch back to **Embedded Live Demo Mode**. The app will immediately operate locally with full features, schedules, and reports.

## 🔒 Security & Data Privacy Assurance
- **Authenticity Verification:** Always verify that the file downloaded is named `North-Poblacion-CleanTrack-v1.0.0.apk` from the official repository.
- **Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173):** Personal phone numbers and home addresses submitted through missed-waste reports are protected and accessible only to authorized Barangay officials and assigned collection supervisors.
- **Ecological Solid Waste Management Act (RA 9003):** The app enforces household waste segregation rules for North Poblacion.
